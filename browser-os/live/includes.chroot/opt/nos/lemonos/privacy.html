Privacy Policy

nOS is operated by lemony. This policy explains what the service stores, where it goes, and how to contact us. Last updated 4 November 2026.

Guest sessions

A guest session keeps a random identifier and native app data in your browser's local storage. That data stays on your device. Clearing your browser's site data removes it irreversibly. Guest sessions do not create an account with us.

Signing in

Sign-in uses Firebase Authentication. Google sign-in shares your name, email address, and profile picture so the desktop can greet you and label your storage. Email sign-in requires a verification link. We derive a storage identifier from your Firebase account and do not receive your password. We cannot read your Gmail or Drive data and request no scopes beyond basic profile and email.

A small session record is kept in your browser so you stay signed in. Guest sessions remain available and keep their data in this browser.

Native app data

Notes, saved code, uploaded files, App Store metadata, and preferences from native apps are stored in Cloudflare Workers KV under a key derived from your account identifier when you are signed in. Guest data stays in local storage. This data is not indexed, is not shown to other users, and is not used to train anything. The service applies a 50 MB per-account quota and a 10 MB limit for a single item. LemonChat messages are stored by Hearth, a separate service, under your Hearth account.

Embedded apps

Writer, Sheets, Slides, Draw, Photo Editor, Video Editor, and the ArtCraft Suite open editors from their own hosts inside a sandboxed frame. The self-hosted ArtCraft editors load from GitHub Pages; Draw can switch to Excalidraw, Photo Editor to Photopea, and Video Editor runs on Omniclip. LemonChat opens Hearth, a separate chat service, and SourBlocks runs from its own host. Opening one sends a request to that host and gives your browser a document from it. Files, messages, and game data are handled by that app and its host, not by nOS storage. Closing the window removes the frame.

Advertising

Google AdSense runs only on the public landing page. AdSense may set cookies or read device identifiers there under Google's own policies. No ad unit is placed inside an app window or inside the desktop. The AdSense loader may remain in the page after you enter the desktop, but no ads are requested there.

Local storage and cookies

The desktop uses local storage for your session and for a local cache so an interrupted connection does not lose your work. We set no first-party tracking cookies. Third-party cookies, if any, come from AdSense on the landing page and from embedded apps when you open them; your browser settings control them.

Third parties

These services can see network requests from the project:

  • Cloudflare — hosting, Workers KV storage, and request logs.
  • Google — Firebase Authentication, Google Fonts, and AdSense on the landing page.
  • GitHub Pages — hosting the self-hosted ArtCraft editors.
  • Excalidraw, Photopea, and Omniclip — optional editors you can open.
  • gstatic — delivery of the Firebase JavaScript SDK.
  • Hearth — LemonChat messages and chat account.
  • SourBlocks — the downloadable game and its own host.
  • Zoho Mail — handling email sent to our contact address.

The App Store catalog is public and is fetched from our API even for guest sessions. We do not embed an analytics product in the desktop.

Deleting your data

Settings includes an export button that produces a JSON copy of the data stored for you, and a delete button that removes the Workers KV keys belonging to your account identifier. The delete action removes the Workers KV keys belonging to your account identifier. Sign-in is client-side and creates no separate nOS account record.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update the date on this page. Questions about data handling can be sent to contact@lemonys.dpdns.org.
